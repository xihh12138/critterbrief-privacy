CritterBrief Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Applies to the CritterBrief Android app (package com.critterbrief.app)

CritterBrief is a small-pet habitat monitoring app. It works together with a cage monitor to show the conditions inside your pet's cage and to give you a short daily brief. This page explains what data the app and the service handle, why, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

CritterBrief is developed and operated by an individual developer. For any privacy question or request, contact us at x1196495689@gmail.com or open an issue at github.com/xihh12138.

Data we collect

  • Monitor and sensor data: device identifier, temperature, humidity, pressure, air-quality (VOC) readings, device status, timestamps and firmware version.
  • Pet profile: the name, species and any notes you choose to enter about your pet.
  • Account data (only if you create an account): your email address and sign-in identifier.
  • Diagnostics: app version, Android version, device model, crash logs and error reports used to keep the app working.

How we use it

  • To show your monitor's readings and produce your daily brief.
  • To send alerts when the cage climate or air quality leaves the comfortable range.
  • To keep the device and account working (pairing, status, firmware updates).
  • To fix bugs and improve reliability, using aggregated diagnostics.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and we do not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location. The app does not request these permissions.

Storage and sharing

Monitor data is stored on the developer's server (and/or a cloud hosting provider used to operate the service). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS / MQTTS) where the service supports them. The app does not share data with third parties except:

  • service providers that host the monitoring service and this policy page (for example, a cloud host and GitHub Pages);
  • when required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of users.

Retention

  • Raw sensor readings: up to 12 months.
  • Daily summaries: up to 24 months.
  • Crash and diagnostic logs: up to 90 days.
  • Account and pet profile: until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and deletion

You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by emailing x1196495689@gmail.com. When the app supports accounts, it will also provide a "Delete my data" entry in the app settings. Deleting your account removes your profile and stops further collection; alert history may be kept in anonymised form for service reliability.

Children

CritterBrief is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted transport, access controls and regular updates to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for material changes, announce it in the app or in the store listing.

萌宠简报隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日 · 适用于萌宠简报 Android 应用（包名 com.critterbrief.app）

萌宠简报（CritterBrief）是一款笼养小宠的窝内环境监测 App。它配合笼上的监测设备，显示笼内温湿度、空气质量和设备状态，并每天给出一份简短小结。本页说明 App 和服务会处理哪些数据、为什么处理，以及你能做什么。

我们是谁

萌宠简报由个人开发者开发和运营。任何隐私相关的问题或请求，可以发邮件到 x1196495689@gmail.com，或在 github.com/xihh12138 提交 issue。

我们收集哪些数据

  • 设备和传感器数据：设备编号、温度、湿度、气压、异味（VOC）读数、设备状态、时间戳和固件版本。
  • 宠物资料：你自愿填写的宠物名字、品种和备注。
  • 账号数据（只有你注册账号时）：邮箱地址和登录标识。
  • 诊断数据：App 版本、Android 版本、设备型号、崩溃日志和错误报告，用来保证 App 正常运行。

我们用这些数据做什么

  • 显示设备的读数，生成每天的小结。
  • 当笼内环境或空气质量超出舒适区间时发送提醒。
  • 保证设备配对、在线状态和固件更新正常工作。
  • 用汇总后的诊断信息修 bug、提高稳定性。

我们不会做什么

  • 不显示广告，不接入第三方广告 SDK。
  • 不买卖你的数据。
  • 不访问摄像头、麦克风、联系人或位置；App 不申请这些权限。

数据存在哪里、分享给谁

监测数据保存在开发者自己的服务器（和/或用于运行服务的云主机）上。服务支持加密连接时，数据通过 HTTPS / MQTTS 传输。除以下情况外，App 不会把数据分享给第三方：

  • 托管监测服务和本页面的服务商（例如云主机和 GitHub Pages）；
  • 法律要求，或为保护用户安全和权利所必需时。

保存多久

  • 原始传感器读数：最多 12 个月。
  • 每日小结：最多 24 个月。
  • 崩溃和诊断日志：最多 90 天。
  • 账号和宠物资料：保存到你要求删除为止。

你的选择和删除方式

你可以随时发邮件到 x1196495689@gmail.com 索取或删除数据。App 支持账号后会同时在设置里提供“删除我的数据”入口。删除账号会移除你的资料并停止收集；为排查服务问题，提醒历史可能以匿名形式保留。

儿童

萌宠简报不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的个人数据。如果你发现儿童提供了数据，请联系我们删除。

安全

我们使用加密传输、访问控制和定期更新来保护数据。任何传输或存储方式都无法做到绝对安全，我们无法保证百分之百安全。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新页面顶部的日期；重大变化会在 App 内或商店页面说明。
